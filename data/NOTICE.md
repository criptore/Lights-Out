# Notices: Storm Blackout and Restoration, version 1.2

Night light data: NASA Black Marble (VNP46A2, VJ146A2), courtesy of NASA LAADS DAAC. Roman et al. (2018), DOI 10.1016/j.rse.2018.03.017.

Utility outage data: EAGLE-I / ORNL, Figshare DOI 10.6084/m9.figshare.24237376 (CC BY 4.0).

Customer estimates: U.S. Energy Information Administration Form EIA-861 and U.S. Census Bureau county housing unit estimates (public domain).

Declarations, boundaries and tracks: OpenFEMA, U.S. Census Bureau cartographic boundary files and NOAA NHC best tracks (public domain).

Not for real-time emergency response or for any decision about one address or property. Provided as is, without warranty of any kind.

This dataset is published by Heimdall Research under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license (CC BY 4.0). The license covers Heimdall Research's contribution. Inputs keep their own terms, listed below.

## Inputs and their terms

- **NASA Black Marble VNP46A2 collection 2 (VIIRS on Suomi NPP), DOI 10.5067/VIIRS/VNP46A2.002** (Night light): NASA Earth science data use policy (free and open). Cite Roman, M.O. et al. (2018), NASA's Black Marble nighttime lights product suite, Remote Sensing of Environment 210, 113-143, DOI 10.1016/j.rse.2018.03.017. Attribution: Cite the product DOI and NASA LAADS DAAC
- **NASA Black Marble VJ146A2 collection 2 (VIIRS on NOAA-20), DOI 10.5067/VIIRS/VJ146A2.002** (Night light, storms measured with NOAA-20): NASA Earth science data use policy (free and open). Cite Roman et al. (2018) as above. Attribution: Cite the product DOI and NASA LAADS DAAC
- **EAGLE-I county outage records, Oak Ridge National Laboratory (Figshare DOI 10.6084/m9.figshare.24237376)** (Customers out, customer-hours and restore times (2014-11-01 onward)): CC BY 4.0
- **FEMA disaster declarations (OpenFEMA)** (Which storms, counties and tribal areas are included, incident begin dates, and `declared_via`): Public domain
- **U.S. Census Bureau cartographic boundary files cb_2024_us_county_500k, cb_2020_us_county_500k and cb_2024_us_aiannh_500k (2020 and 2024 vintages)** (County, Connecticut 2020 county and tribal-area boundaries. Hashes in census_boundaries_manifest.json): Public domain
- **NOAA National Hurricane Center best tracks: HURDAT2 Atlantic (hurdat2-1851-2025-091226.txt) and Northeast and North Central Pacific (hurdat2-nepac-1949-2025-092926.txt)** (Landfall dates and times, county impact dates, 34-knot wind radii. Files in nhc_files_manifest.json): Public domain
- **U.S. Energy Information Administration Form EIA-861 detailed data files, https://www.eia.gov/electricity/data/eia861/** (Customers by state and utility, the base of customers_total): Public domain
- **U.S. Census Bureau county housing unit estimates (vintages 2020 and 2024)** (Splits state customer counts across counties): Public domain
